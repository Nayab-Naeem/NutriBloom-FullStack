# 🌸 NutriBloom

> **An AI-powered nutrition and meal tracking application that helps users understand their nutritional needs, track meals, and receive personalized food recommendations based on their health goals.**

NutriBloom is a full-stack nutrition and meal tracking web application built with **React, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Node.js, Express.js, and Google Gemini AI**.

The application calculates personalized health metrics such as **BMI, BMR, and TDEE**, determines a suitable nutrition goal, generates daily calorie and macronutrient targets, and provides AI-powered food analysis and meal suggestions.

Users can follow one of three goal modes — **Weight Gain, Weight Loss, or Weight Maintenance** — and track their daily nutrition through an interactive dashboard.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👤 Personalized Onboarding

NutriBloom provides a personalized onboarding experience where users enter:

- Age
- Gender
- Height
- Weight
- Activity level

The application then calculates:

- **BMI (Body Mass Index)**
- **BMI Category**
- **BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)**
- **TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)**
- Daily calorie target
- Daily protein target
- Daily carbohydrate target
- Daily fat target
- Recommended nutrition goal

---

### 🎯 Goal-Based Nutrition Modes

NutriBloom supports three personalized nutrition modes:

#### 💪 Weight Gain

Focuses on a healthy calorie surplus and nutritious, calorie-dense food recommendations.

#### 🔥 Weight Loss

Focuses on a moderate calorie deficit, lighter food options, and filling meals.

#### ⚖️ Weight Maintenance

Provides balanced nutrition recommendations designed to help maintain a healthy lifestyle.

The dashboard and AI recommendations adapt according to the user's selected goal.

---

### 🤖 AI-Powered Food Analysis

Users can describe a food or meal in natural language, and Google Gemini AI estimates its nutritional information.

The AI can estimate:

- Calories
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Fat
- Individual food items
